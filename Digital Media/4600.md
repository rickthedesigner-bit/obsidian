# Description and details

DPF 4600GLX/MLX is your go-to high performance calendered film providing you with an efficient and versatile solution. DPF 4600GLX/MLX can be used with or without an overlaminate while maintaining the perfect balance of body and conformability. The repositionable light gray adhesive allows for easy installation while ensuring enough opacity for covering existing graphics. The LX (Low Profile) X-Scape Technology® provides installers a bubble-free installation with a smooth, paint-like finish for a multitude of different applications from vehicle decals, to promotional signage, and interior wall. Depend on DPF 4600GLX/MLX to deliver the best results.

_DPF 4600GLX/MLX is designed for use on simple to moderate curves for vehicle wraps._
