## Overview

#### 3M™ Controltac™ Graphic Film with Comply™ v3 Adhesive IJ180Cv3 is a versatile, conformable 2 mil film with slideable, repositionable, and medium/higher tack. It features Comply™ v3 Adhesive for fast, easy, and bubble-free installation. Use this long term, removable, inkjet printable film for signs, fleet graphics, full vehicle wraps, and watercraft. Recommended Graphic Protection: 8518, 8418G, 8519, 8520, 8915, 8528, 8548G, 8549L, 8550M, 9740i, 9760LX, 1920DR
