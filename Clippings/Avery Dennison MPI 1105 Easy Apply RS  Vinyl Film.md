## Overview

#### Avery Dennison MPI 1105 EZ RS is a removable, highly conformable SuperCast vinyl film engineered for vehicle wraps, complex curves, and irregular surface applications. Featuring Easy Apply™ air-release technology and a repositionable slide (RS) adhesive for precise placement, this film delivers up to 7-year outdoor durability with excellent dimensional stability for clean cutting and converting. MPI 1105 must be paired with Avery Dennison DOL 1000Z, 1300Z, or 1400Z series overlaminate for graphic protection. Available in stock and ships from U.S. distribution warehouses nationwide. Sold wholesale to sign shops, wrap installers, print shops, and trade buyers.
