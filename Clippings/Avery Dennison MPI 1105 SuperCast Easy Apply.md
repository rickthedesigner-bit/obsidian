## Overview

#### Avery Dennison MPI 1105 SuperCast vinyl is a removable, highly conformable film designed to be a solution for wrapping and irregular surface applications. The Easy Apply™ technology uses air release to reduce wrinkles and bubbles during installation. SuperCast vinyl has outstanding durability and outdoor performance with an expected durability of up to 7 years, as well as excellent dimensional stability that allows it to be easily converted. For graphic protection, MPI 1105 must be paired with Avery DOL 1000Z, 1300Z or 1400Z series laminates.
