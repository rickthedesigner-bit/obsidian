## Series 3510

Every graphic deserves to be center stage, and a short-term graphic is no different. Series 3510 is a 3.2-mil (80 micron) calendered printable film with a clear, permanent pressure-sensitive adhesive. This printable overlaminate offers high-quality printability and is coated on a two-sided polycoated liner that provides excellent lay-flat properties. Series 3510 is rated for outdoor durability of 3 years.
