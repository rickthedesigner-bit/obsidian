## Overview

#### 3M™ Print Wrap Film IJ180mC is a versatile, conformable 2 mil film with slideable, repositionable, medium/higher tack. It features Comply™ Adhesive with micro technology for fast, easy, bubble-free installation with a smooth appearance. Use this long term, removable, inkjet printable film for signs, fleet graphics, full vehicle wraps, and watercraft. Recommended Graphics Protection: 8518, 8519, 8520, 8915, 8528, 8548G, 8549L, 8550M, 9740i, 9760LX, 1920DR
