## Series 3350 Anti-Graffiti

Series 3350 is a PVDF film with clear, permanent pressure-sensitive adhesive. Along with protecting and prolonging the life of printed images, Series 3350 is designed as an anti-graffiti overlaminate. Used as an overlaminate, the outdoor durability is rated up to 10 years. The color fastness of prints can be extended up to 2 years beyond the ink manufacturer’s warranty when following print recommendations by the ink and printer manufacturers.

#### Unique Features

- Protecting your Graphics in Vandalism Prone Areas
- Easy to Clean with Most Traditional Cleaning Methods
- Extended Life Overlaminate
