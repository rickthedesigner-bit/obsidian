## Overview

#### Designed to be more durable and much lighter than glass, ACRYLITE® Extruded Acrylic offers a high-quality solution where safety and ease of handling are factors. This acrylic has a low molecular weight and is great for general signage, illuminated signage, thermoforming, framing, and making displays. With high impact strength and an enhanced level of flexibility, ACRYLITE® Extruded is made in the USA and designed to be durable while producing consistent results. For UL listing, compliance and certification information, please visit the ACRYLITE® Technology Center. This material is not digitally printable. For printable sheets, check out ACRYLITE® Extruded Acrylic-Digital Print.
