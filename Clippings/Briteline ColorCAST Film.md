## Overview

#### Briteline ColorCAST is Grimco's exclusive cast color change vinyl, engineered for automotive restyling and fleet applications. Featuring the same trusted adhesive technology as the original Briteline WrapCAST, ColorCAST offers the repositionability and deep channel confidence installers already know and rely on. The conformable cast film delivers fast, easy installation with excellent repositionability and clean removability, with zero adhesive residue left behind. The Invisible Air Channel Liner ensures a bubble-free, ultra-smooth finish on full and partial vehicle wraps, while the lay-flat polycoated liner eliminates glue lines after just a few hours of exposure. A PET protective cap sheet on all gloss colors prevents orange peel for the highest gloss level available. Guaranteed to stay down in deep channels, curves, recesses, and the most demanding shapes, and offers excellent heat recovery. Briteline ColorCAST launches with 14 eye-catching colors in gloss, satin, and matte finishes, all sold by the yard. To purchase Briteline ColorCAST by the yard, select the 1yd option and adjust the cart quantity to the appropriate length (up to 25yd). 1yd increments may not be continuous. By the yard sales are final.Note: When ordering different size cut rolls, please place a separate order for each size. Note: All ColorCAST gloss films have a clear protective film layer to prevent orange peel and scratching; this protective film layer must be removed BEFORE installation.
