## Premium Cast Overlaminate

When has too many options ever been a bad thing? Series 3220 is a premium cast protective overlaminate available in Gloss, Matte, Luster or Satin finish to help customize the look of your graphic. With UV inhibitors protecting your graphic from weathering and added dimensional stability, you’ll have an easier graphic installation and clean removal at the end.

### Unique Features

- Conformable Overlaminate Available in Multiple Finishes to Customize your Graphics
- Compatible with Most Digitally Printable Products
- Added UV Stabilizers to Enhance Life of Graphics
